How It Works

THE PROCESS.

Every vehicle goes through the same meticulous process. No shortcuts, no rushing. This is how we protect your investment.

01
Enquiry & Quote

Call or submit a form. We assess your vehicle's needs, recommend a package, and give you a transparent quote. No hidden costs.

02
Wash & Prep

Full decontamination wash, iron fallout removal, clay bar treatment. The surface must be perfect before anything else begins.

03
Paint Correction

For Full Correction packages — we machine polish every panel to remove swirls, scratches and oxidation. Hours of work, zero compromise.

04
Coating & Cure

Magnum Ceramic Coating is applied panel by panel, flashed, levelled and cured. Your warranty certificate is issued on completion.
