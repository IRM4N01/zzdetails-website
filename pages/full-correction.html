ABOUT THIS PACKAGE

The Full Correction & Coating package is our flagship service — designed for vehicles that need serious paint restoration before a ceramic coating goes on. Swirls, scratches, water marks, and oxidation are meticulously removed through a multi-stage machine polishing process before Magnum ceramic coating is applied.

This is the package for car owners who want their vehicle looking better than the day it left the dealership — and staying that way for years. No shortcuts, no corners cut. Countless hours of careful work go into every job.

THE PROCESS

01

Full Detail & Decontamination

Complete hand wash, iron decontamination, clay bar and full prep to remove all surface contaminants.

02

Multi-Stage Paint Correction

Machine polishing in multiple stages to remove swirls, scratches, oxidation and water marks.

03

Coating Application

Magnum ceramic coating applied panel by panel to the fully corrected paintwork.

04

Inspection & Handover

Full post-coating inspection, care guide provided, and warranty certificate issued.
