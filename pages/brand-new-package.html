ABOUT THIS PACKAGE

The Brand New Car Package is designed for new or near-new vehicles that don't require heavy paint correction — just a thorough prep and professional ceramic coating application. Perfect if you want to protect your investment from day one before environmental damage, UV, and contaminants take hold.

Every vehicle is hand-washed and decontaminated before the coating goes on — we never skip prep work, because a coating applied to a dirty or improperly prepared surface won't bond correctly. You get what you pay for here.

THE PROCESS

01

Wash & Decontamination

Full hand wash, iron decontamination and clay bar treatment to remove bonded contaminants.

02

Paint Prep & Polish

Surface is polished to remove any light imperfections and prepped for coating adhesion.

03

Coating Application

Magnum ceramic coating is applied panel by panel in a controlled environment.

04

Inspection & Handover

Full post-coating inspection, care guide provided, and warranty issued.
